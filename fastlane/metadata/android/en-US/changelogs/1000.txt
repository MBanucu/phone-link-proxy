0.10.0

- Save as… copies a proxy under a new name and opens the copy; a name that is
  taken is asked about first.
- All events shows every proxy's events, and says so when the log is empty.
- Rename moves a proxy's settings, its switches and its in-use mark together.
- Each control has an info mark, and About this app documents the adb contract.
- A stopped service publishes the few facts a check needs in a state file that
  holds no credentials.
